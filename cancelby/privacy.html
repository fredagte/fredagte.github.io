2. Grundsatz

CancelBy erinnert dich rechtzeitig daran, kostenlose Testzeiträume und Abos zu kündigen. Es gibt kein Benutzerkonto und keine eigenen Server. Wir erhalten keine Daten über dich oder deine Einträge. Die App enthält kein Analyse- und kein Absturzberichts-Tool.

3. Daten, die nur auf deinem Gerät gespeichert werden

  • Deine Einträge: Name des Dienstes, Datumsangaben, Preis, Währung, Kündigungslink, Notiz und Status.
  • Einstellungen (z. B. Erinnerungszeit, Design, Währung) und der Kaufstatus von CancelBy Pro.

Diese Daten verlassen dein Gerät nicht in Richtung des Anbieters dieser App. Unter Android werden sie nicht in Sicherungen übernommen. Unter iOS können sie in eine von dir eingerichtete Gerätesicherung (iCloud oder Computer) aufgenommen werden. Eine CSV-Datei (Pro) entsteht nur, wenn du den Export startest, und wird nur dorthin weitergegeben, wohin du sie selbst teilst. Du kannst alle Daten jederzeit in der App oder durch Deinstallieren der App löschen. Erinnerungen sind lokale Mitteilungen deines Geräts; dafür wird nichts an Server gesendet.

4. Kündigungslinks

2. Summary

CancelBy reminds you to cancel free trials and subscriptions in time. There is no user account and we operate no servers. We do not receive any data about you or your entries. The app contains no analytics and no crash-reporting tool.

3. Data stored only on your device

  • Your entries: service name, dates, price, currency, cancel link, note and status.
  • Settings (e.g. reminder time, theme, currency) and your CancelBy Pro purchase status.

This data never leaves your device towards the developer of this app. On Android it is excluded from backups. On iOS it may be included in a device backup you set up (iCloud or computer). A CSV file (Pro) is created only when you start an export and goes only where you share it. You can delete all data at any time in the app or by uninstalling it. Reminders are local notifications of your device; nothing is sent to a server for them.

4. Cancel links

The app can show links to the cancellation pages of well-known services. When you open one, that website's operator's privacy policy applies. CancelBy is not affiliated with these services.

5. Purchases through Google Play and the App Store

CancelBy Pro (monthly or yearly subscription, or one-time purchase) is handled by your device's store: on Android by Google Play Billing (Google Ireland Limited, policies.google.com/privacy), on iOS by the App Store (Apple Distribution International Ltd., apple.com/legal/privacy). The app only learns whether and which product was bought and stores this status on the device. You manage and cancel subscriptions in your Google or Apple account.

6. Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows ads via Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). Device identifiers (e.g. the advertising ID or app set ID on Android, a vendor identifier on iOS), your IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), device and app information, diagnostics and ad interactions may be sent to Google for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we first ask for your consent through Google's consent dialog (User Messaging Platform) (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent, at most non-personalised or limited ads are shown. On iOS the app also asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency); the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it. You can change your choice at any time in the app's settings (privacy options) or in iOS Settings. CancelBy Pro loads no ads. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

7. Permissions

  • Internet: ads, consent dialog and purchases.
  • Notifications: reminders before your trials end.
  • Android – run at startup: reschedule reminders after the device restarts.
  • iOS – tracking (only when asked, see section 6).

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. As we do not store personal data about you, please contact Google or Apple directly for data they hold. Questions about this policy: freddyleather@gmail.com.
